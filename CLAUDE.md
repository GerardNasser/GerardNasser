## Design

This site does NOT use the UNC Charlotte brand. Do not introduce the
Charlotte palette, typefaces or marker.

Brand: the site's own
Palette: as defined in site/src/styles/tokens.css
Type: Bricolage Grotesque (display), JetBrains Mono (labels, meta, UI, code), Spectral (ledes, taglines)
Tone: terminal-dark, scientific

Existing styles are the design system. Match them. Craft rules
(contrast, typography, chart validation, QA) still apply.
